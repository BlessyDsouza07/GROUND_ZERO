## 2. Who can use GroundZero
GroundZero accounts are for people aged **18 or over**. We ask you to confirm this at sign-up. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has an account, contact the Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

## 3. What we collect, and why
| Data | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| Email, password (stored only as a hash), display name | Your account | At sign-up |
| Trips, saved places, journal notes | The features you use | When you create them |
| **Approximate/precise location** | Show what's open and near you; "near this place" nudges | **Only if you switch location on.** Your device also asks permission. |
| **Personalisation memory** (interests we infer from your taps, saves, skips; your constraints such as diet or budget) | Better suggestions | **Only if you switch personalisation on** |
| Photos you add | Your journal, trip story, or a place you contribute | Only the photos you choose. Camera access is asked on use. |
| Reports you file | Safety and moderation | When you press Report |

We do **not** use your data for advertising and we do **not** sell it.

## 4. Location, specifically
* Location is requested only after you agree in the app and in your browser or phone.
* Location is processed to answer your request. **We do not store a history of where you have been.** For nudges, we only keep, per place, whether you were near it and when we last told you, so we don't repeat ourselves.
* You can pause or turn off location at any time in Privacy. Turning it off deletes that per-place state.

## 5. Photos
Photos are re-saved without their hidden metadata (including GPS position and camera details). A photo you attach to a *place contribution* may be shown to others once approved. Please don't photograph people without their consent, and don't include faces of minors, vehicle numbers or private homes. You can report any photo.

## 6. Who we share data with
Service providers that run GroundZero (hosting, and the AI/voice features). When you use the Guide, your message text (and, if you allow, your coarse location) may be sent to our AI provider (Groq) and, for speech, to a text-to-speech service, to produce an answer. Do not put sensitive personal details in Guide questions. We disclose data when required by law.
Maps tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers, which can see your IP address.

## 7. How long we keep data
* Account data: until you delete your account.
* Personalisation memory and feedback events: until you delete them, withdraw consent, or delete your account.
* Reports: [12] months after closure, then deleted.
* Backups: removed within [30] days of deletion.

## 8. Your rights
You can, inside the app (Privacy): see and edit what GroundZero has learned about you; export your data; withdraw any consent; delete the learned memory; delete your account and all its data. You may also nominate someone, request correction, or raise a grievance with the Grievance Officer; if unresolved you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## 9. Security
Passwords are hashed. Connections should use HTTPS. Uploaded files get random names. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting you occurs we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 10. Changes
We will show you a notice when this policy materially changes and ask again where consent is needed.
