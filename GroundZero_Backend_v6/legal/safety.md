# Stay safe in Mangaluru

**Emergency numbers (India):** 112 all emergencies · 108 ambulance · 100 police · 1363 tourist helpline · 1091 women's helpline.

* **Sea:** From June to September the Arabian Sea is rough. Stay out of the water, keep back from the edge, obey lifeguards and flags.
* **Heat and rain:** Carry water in summer. In heavy rain avoid low roads, rocks and viewpoints.
* **After dark:** Prefer well-lit, busy places. Share your plan with someone you trust.
* **Temples, mosques, churches:** Dress modestly, remove footwear where asked, ask before taking photos.
* **Photos:** Don't photograph strangers, children or private homes without permission.
* **Hours:** If GroundZero says "typically", call ahead; we haven't confirmed it.

GroundZero is not an emergency service.
